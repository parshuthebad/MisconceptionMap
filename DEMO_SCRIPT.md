# 60-second demo video script (screen-record on phone or laptop)

| Time | Show | Say |
|------|------|-----|
| 0:00-0:08 | Title + a wrong handwritten solution on paper | "Most AI tutors only check the final answer. Students need to know *why* they're wrong." |
| 0:08-0:25 | Open app, take photo, pick Hindi, tap mic and explain | "I photograph my work and explain my thinking in my own language." |
| 0:25-0:40 | Result: red circle on step 3, misconception tag, hint | "It finds the exact line, names the misconception, and gives a hint, not the answer." |
| 0:40-0:48 | Tap "Read feedback aloud" | (let the Hindi voice play) |
| 0:48-0:58 | Teacher tab -> Load sample class | "Teachers see which misconception hits most of the class, so they know what to re-teach." |
| 0:58-1:00 | Accuracy number from eval | "On 40 real solutions it located the first error X% of the time."|

Tips: record in Chrome, use a real handwritten page with one clear mistake, keep it under 90 seconds.
